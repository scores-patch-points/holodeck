The "tautology" (Allen / Rosenberg)
Council Member Allen, 02:24:14:
"We had a speaker at the beginning of the meeting that asked ... could we add a sentence that says anything that would be acquired that is subject to Section 13808, which is the surveillance section, would have to comply with that section. And so I would like to ask legal to respond. If something is subject to 1308, it would have to comply with that section. Is there any circumstance where it's a small enough addition to the contract that it would slide under ... by passing this resolution, if there is an addition made later that is subject to that ...?"

Dave Rosenberg (mayor's office), 02:26:07:
"Please correct me if I'm answering the wrong question. You cannot use this contract for things covered under the surveillance code because to approve something under the surveillance code, it requires a public hearing for approval. Rosenberg again, 02:27:30:
"Yes, because in order to approve any surveillance use or a contract that includes surveillance uses, it has to be passed by resolution with a public hearing before council there. I mean, by definition, by not having a public hearing, there is no opportunity to do that."
